# Founder project images

The two WebP images in this folder are **optimized renditions of the two screenshots supplied in the founder's chat**. They are not replacement illustrations or stock photos. The original high-resolution PNG files are preserved separately in the chat's downloadable `founder-project-original-screenshots.zip` archive and may replace these renditions when a binary upload is available.

- `openmindai-desktop.webp`: 640 × 343, derived only by resizing and WebP encoding of `image(20261019-071617).png`.
- `openmind-studio-desktop.webp`: 640 × 345, derived only by resizing and WebP encoding of `image(20261019-071623).png`.

For full-resolution images later, copy both original PNGs into this folder and update `frontend/lib/founder-projects.js` image paths and dimensions to point to the PNG files.
